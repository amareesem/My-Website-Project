Six months with your own physician.

Two things happen at once: a personal clinical relationship, and a structured education that changes how you live. About an hour a week, plus one virtual visit each month.

  1. TrailheadOne hour, one to one, with Dr. Lori or Dr. Chris.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2At-home testing. The factors shaping your brain, from your own blood and biology.
  3. Weeks 3 to 4Review of Findings. A protocol designed for you alone.
  4. Months 2 to 7Six Basecamps, then graduation, with the option to keep climbing in PEAK Ascent.
