Six months, one physician, one step at a time.

About an hour a week for the module, and a one to one virtual visit with Dr. Lori or Dr. Chris every month. Anytime secure messaging in between.

  2. At-home testing Weeks 1 to 2. Answers from your own blood, genes, and biology.
