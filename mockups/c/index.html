After 62 years in family medicine, we each lost a parent to dementia. We did everything conventional medicine taught us. It was not enough, because what we had been taught was incomplete.

So we went back to learning. We retrained in root-cause and longevity medicine, applied it to ourselves first, and built PEAK so other families would have what ours never did: answers early enough to change the story.

PEAK is everything we wish our parents had.

What the six months look like.

A personal clinical relationship, and a short weekly module. About an hour of your time, inside the life you already have. One virtual visit with your physician each month.

Southlake, Texas, and by video nationwide. Often before any memory problem shows.

Trailhead. One hour with Dr. Lori or Dr. Chris. $350, credited toward the program if you enroll. A spouse's consultation is free when booked together.

Then the testing, mostly from home, and a Review of Findings that becomes a protocol for you alone. Six Basecamps follow. You leave a graduate, and PEAK Ascent is there if you want another year.
