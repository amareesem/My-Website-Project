Questions people ask before booking

Questions, answered honestly.

Clear answers about the PEAK program, testing, time, and what to expect.

Do I need to have memory problems to start?

No. The best time is before anything shows. Most PEAK patients come because they want to keep it that way, often after watching a parent decline.

Why can't I just ask my regular doctor about this?

For many of you, we are your regular doctor, which is why we can answer this honestly. A standard fifteen-minute visit billed to insurance was never built to include four-marker brain biomarker testing, a full root-cause work-up, or six months of physician time. That is a limitation of the payment model, not of the physician. PEAK exists as a separate program to do the work a normal visit cannot.

What is cognitive longevity?

Cognitive longevity is the practice of supporting how your brain functions for as long as possible. It means finding and working on measurable factors, often years before symptoms appear, instead of waiting for a diagnosis.

How is this different from a traditional doctor's visit?

A traditional visit is short, symptom-driven, and built around insurance billing codes. PEAK is six months long, starts with advanced testing rather than a checklist of complaints, and gives you direct, ongoing access to the physician building your protocol.

Do I have to live near Southlake? How does the virtual program work?

No. PEAK is built to work virtually. Testing is done largely from home, and your monthly visits with Dr. Lori or Dr. Chris happen over video. Patients join us from outside Southlake regularly. Call us to confirm availability in your state.

Why isn't this covered by insurance?

Insurance pays to treat disease once it is diagnosed. It does not pay for the advanced testing, protocol development, or physician time this early work takes. Filing insurance would mean building PEAK around what a payer approves rather than what the evidence supports, and we were not willing to do that.

What if I'm already a Timarron Family Medicine patient?

PEAK is a separate program from your regular Timarron Family Medicine care, with its own testing, protocol, and schedule of visits. Being an existing patient does not change the cost or process, but it does mean you are already working with a physician you know.

How much time does this take each week?

About an hour a week for the module itself. That is the reading and thinking it through. The action steps live inside the life you already have: what you eat, how you sleep, how you move, rather than added on top of it. You also have one virtual visit with your physician each month.

What happens at the consultation, and why does it cost $350?

The consultation is a full hour, one to one, with Dr. Lori or Dr. Chris. Not a coordinator, and not a sales call. It is the first hour of the work itself. The $350 is credited in full toward your program if you enroll, and it is refundable up to 48 hours before your appointment.

Can my spouse and I do this together?

Yes. Book two consultations back to back on the same day and the second consultation is free. If you both enroll, each person receives $500 off, even if you choose different tiers. Everything else remains separate: your own testing, physician visits, and protocol.

What is the health history form, and how long does it take?

It is a detailed intake covering ground a standard visit never touches: sleep, stress, diet, exposures, medications, and family patterns. It takes about thirty minutes and you will complete it before your consultation.

I carry APOE4 or have a parent with dementia. Is it too late?

Genes load the dice. Biology and lifestyle shape how they roll. Dr. Lori carries very high genetic risk herself, and has lowered her biological age by more than 10 years.

What if a result is hard to hear?

Every result arrives with a plan and a physician beside you. We look for risks so we can work on them, not to deliver a verdict.

What happens after the six months?

You finish with a PEAK Graduation Session: a full look at what changed and what is next. Most graduates continue with us into PEAK Ascent, our year-two program, but that is a conversation for after you have seen your own results, not before.

Do I have to take supplements?

Not necessarily. Your protocol is built around what your testing actually shows. For some patients that includes targeted supplementation. For others the priority is sleep, nutrition, or movement. Your physician will walk you through what is recommended for you and why.

Am I too late for this to help?

The earlier you start, the more you can change. The risk factors that drive cognitive decline remain modifiable into the seventies and eighties, and blood pressure, blood sugar, sleep, hearing, and inflammation still respond at any age. What changes with age is not whether this work is useful. It is what we prioritize, and how quickly we want to see the numbers move.
