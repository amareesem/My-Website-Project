Why brain health deserves your attention

You may have heard that dementia is only genes or bad luck, and that nothing can be done until memory slips. The science is more hopeful than that.

Of the known risk factors for dementia, more than 80% are modifiable, and the Lancet Commission estimates that addressing just 14 of them could prevent or delay up to 45% of dementia cases worldwide.

The brain changes behind Alzheimer's can begin as many as 20 years before the first forgotten name. Your 40s, 50s, and 60s are a useful window, not a waiting room.
