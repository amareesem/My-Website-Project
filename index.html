The story most of us were told is incomplete.

You have probably heard what we were taught in medical school: dementia is genetic, it is bad luck, and nothing can be done until memory starts slipping. The science now tells a different story. Of the known risk factors for dementia, more than 80% are modifiable, and the Lancet Commission estimates that addressing just 14 of them could prevent or delay up to 45% of dementia cases worldwide.

The brain changes behind Alzheimer's can begin as many as 20 years before the first forgotten name. That means your 40s, 50s, and 60s are not a waiting room. They are your window.

Healthspan, not just lifespan

This is about your next 30 years.

Cognitive longevity means being the one who remembers the grandkids' recital, plans the trip, runs the business, finishes the crossword, and is still the person everyone calls for advice. The root causes we address for your brain also show up in energy, sleep, weight, mood, and blood pressure.

There is no single pill for that future. The small choices you make every day matter: how and when you eat, how you move and sleep, the light you see, the toxins around you, how you handle stress, and the gratitude, nature, and connection in your life. Each choice either fuels your brain or quietly drains it.

PEAK puts a plan built for your body in your hands, along with the knowledge to choose with confidence. One choice at a time, the future you just pictured becomes something you build.

A few questions people ask first

Do I need to have memory problems to start?

No. The best time is before anything shows. Most PEAK patients come because they want to keep it that way, often after watching a parent decline.

What if a result is hard to hear?

Every result arrives with a plan and a physician beside you. We look for risks so we can work on them, not to deliver a verdict.

Yes. Your spouse's consultation is free when booked with yours, and if you both enroll, you each receive $500 off.
