Food Fight

Win the battle for your waistline, your health, and your brain.

Weight gain, chronic disease, and memory loss are not three separate problems. They share the same four root causes, and food feeds all four. This free guide shows you how to work on all three from your plate, one small swap at a time.

What's inside

  • Why cutting sugary drinks is the easiest first win, and what changes when you do
  • The leafy-greens habit linked to brains that performed about 11 years younger
  • How to quit sugar, and what to expect in the first week
  • Ultra-processed foods, dementia risk, and the simple 5-ingredient rule
  • Which fish give you brain-building omega-3s without the mercury

About Dr. Lori King

Dr. Lori King, MD, is the founder of PEAK Cognitive Longevity at Timarron Family Medicine in Southlake, Texas. A conventionally trained family physician, she retrained in root-cause and longevity medicine after losing a parent to dementia.

Food Fight is the guide she wishes every patient had: practical, evidence-based, and written so you can actually use it.
