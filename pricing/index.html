Pricing

Make the climb work for you.

PEAK is paid out of pocket. Insurance does not cover this kind of early, root-cause program. We offer clear options so payment never obscures the decision you are making.

Every package includes your Review of Findings, a personalized protocol, six monthly Basecamps with 26 weekly modules, a one-to-one virtual visit with your physician every month, anytime secure messaging, and your PEAK Graduation.
