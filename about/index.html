Our story

Two family doctors. One promise.

For decades we cared for families in Southlake. Then dementia came into our own family, and it changed the kind of doctors we are.

Our story

Between us, we have practiced family medicine for 62 years. We each lost a parent to dementia, and we did everything conventional medicine taught us. It was not enough, because what we had been taught was incomplete.

So we went back to learning. We retrained in root-cause and longevity medicine, applied it to ourselves first, and built PEAK so other families would have what ours never did: answers early enough to change the story.

"I lost 2 people I loved deeply to dementia, and I promised myself I would spend my career making sure that didn't happen to anyone else I could reach."

Dr. Lori King, MD

PEAK was built by Dr. Lori King and Dr. Chris King, the physicians of Timarron Family Medicine in Southlake, Texas. Between them they have more than sixty years in family practice, most of it spent doing conventional medicine well.

Several years ago they both retrained in root-cause and longevity medicine, because the conventional model had no useful answer to the question that mattered most to their own families.

In PEAK, you work directly with one of them.

Not a coach, not a care coordinator, not a protocol delivered by an app. Your own physician, every month, for six months.

Our parents

We know dementia as physicians, and as a daughter and son who watched a parent live with it. We did what we knew to do for our own families, but conventional medicine offered few ways to look for the underlying risks early or a clear path to change them.

That experience changed the questions we asked in our practice. We retrained in root-cause and longevity medicine, and built PEAK to bring families earlier testing, a physician who knows them, and practical steps to support brain health.

Living what we teach

We do not ask our patients to do anything we do not do ourselves. Hiking, cooking real food, protecting our sleep, and time with the people we love: this is what cognitive longevity looks like in real life.

What we believe

Your body was designed to heal.

Given the right conditions, the brain and body repair, renew, and thrive.

Root causes, not symptoms.

We look for the inflammation, blood sugar, toxins, gut, sleep, and hormone issues that can drive decline.

Joy first.

Lifestyle change is life. Small, joyful steps beat willpower and guilt.

We walk beside you.

Your own physician every month, and a team that knows you by name.

PEAK Cognitive Longevity is a physician-led program. We measure what we can, look for root causes, and build a protocol for one person at a time. We do not promise to prevent or reverse dementia. You get physician time, clear testing, and a plan you can follow.

The mind is a gift worth fighting for. This is the climb, and we walk it beside the people we serve.
