Begin that day if it is right

Before we meet, you will complete a detailed health and lifestyle history. It takes about thirty minutes and covers sleep, stress, diet, exposures, medications, family patterns, and the things you have noticed but never mentioned to a doctor.

Then we spend a full hour together, one to one, with a physician. Real questions get real answers.

No pressure, and no obligation. Whether or not you ever enroll, you will leave this hour knowing more about your brain than you ever have: your risk factors, what is already protecting you, and what you can start doing today.

$350, credited in full toward your program if you enroll

Refundable up to 48 hours before your appointment.

Bring the people who matter to you

Decisions about your health are rarely made alone, and they do not have to be. You are warmly welcome to bring your spouse, an adult son or daughter, a trusted friend, or anyone who helps you make important decisions. They can join your consultation, ask their own questions, and hear every answer firsthand, so you can decide together with confidence.

If your spouse would like a consultation of their own, book it back to back with yours on the same day. The second consultation is free.
