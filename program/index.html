Four brain biomarkers, from a blood draw

Most brain-health programs never look directly at the brain. We do, with four blood biomarkers that together give a picture of what is happening in your nervous system right now. They are one part of a larger work-up. Metabolic health, gut, hormones, nutrients, cellular energy, and, depending on your tier, genetics and toxic exposures are measured alongside them because those systems influence what the brain markers eventually show.

  • p-Tau 217: the most reliable blood marker we have for Alzheimer's-type changes in the brain
  • Amyloid beta 42/40 ratio: whether amyloid is accumulating, and to what degree
  • NfL (neurofilament light): a signal of nerve cell injury anywhere in the nervous system
  • GFAP: a measure of inflammation in the brain's support cells

No single one of these tells the whole story. Read together, they tell us whether anything is happening beneath the surface, years before it would show up in how you think or feel.
